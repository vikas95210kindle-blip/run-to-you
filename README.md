# Run to You

A pixel adventure for friends: run, climb, bounce and race through five worlds. Made by Purest for Sunshine.

Play it at **https://vikas95210kindle-blip.github.io/run-to-you/**

- Five worlds with ladders to rooftop roads, springs to sky roads, stairs, lifts, gaps and grumpy puffs to stomp.
- Treasure chests up high, a power bar that fills every 20 hearts, and a flag that marks your farthest run.
- A short love tip at every level gate, matched to your runner (a tip about him for the girls, a tip about her for the boys).
- Play together on up to 5 phones with one room code. Direct phone-to-phone links when possible, revive each other with 3 hearts, race for the crown.

`mqtt.min.js` is the [MQTT.js](https://github.com/mqttjs/MQTT.js) 5.10.1 browser bundle (MIT), used for the live multiplayer mode.
