        Missions

        How to play

        • Tap to jump, hold to jump higher. High hearts need a full jump.
        • Catch hearts in a row for a combo, up to x5. Missing one resets it.
        • Golden hearts are rare: 50 days and 10 ♥ each.
        • Power-ups: Purest's hug saves you from one hit, the love magnet pulls hearts in, and double days doubles every point.
        • Jump onto a ladder to climb to the rooftops. Springs launch you to the sky road. Lifts carry you up and down. Fall off the high road and you simply land on the low road.
        • Land on floating platforms, leap over gaps, and jump on grumpy puffs to stomp them.
        • Every level gate is a checkpoint: you get a life back, and a downed partner comes back to life.
        • Clear a cactus by a whisker for a close-call bonus.
        • Play together on up to 5 phones. Jump at the same moment as someone for an in-sync bonus. If someone goes down, catch 3 hearts to bring them back. Whoever leads wears the crown.
        • Spend ♥ in the wardrobe on new outfits.
